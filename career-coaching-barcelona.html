Situations people bring to career coaching

  • You have been offered a promotion or a move abroad and cannot tell whether you actually want it.
  • You lead a team in your second or third language and feel you only show half of yourself.
  • You came to Barcelona for your partner's job and want to rebuild a professional identity here.
  • You are returning to work after parental leave or a period of exhaustion.
  • Your job has lost its meaning, but leaving it feels reckless.

If you are considering a full change of direction, my guide to changing career while living abroad can help you think it through before we meet.

How I work as a coach

Coaching starts from a goal, or from a question that needs to become one. We clarify what is in the way, then build a realistic path: an action plan, small experiments between sessions, and adjustments as you learn what works. I do not hand out ready-made solutions. In the ICF frame, coaching is a partnership: you know your situation, I bring structure, questions and attention to what keeps repeating.

I have been ICF-certified since 2020, as an Associate Certified Coach (ACC). Coaching is not psychotherapy and does not replace medical or therapeutic support. I am also trained in Gestalt therapy, so if something deeper needs space, we can talk about it openly and choose the right frame. My page on how coaching works explains the method in more detail.

Why my corporate background matters

Before training as a therapist and coach, I spent around twenty years in the corporate world. I started as a journalist at an international television channel, then held leadership roles in large technology companies, working across communication, organisational transformation and change management. Part of that path unfolded in Asia.

I know the codes of international organisations, the pressure of visibility, loyalty conflicts and what it costs to work every day in a culture that is not your own. I have also rebuilt my own professional path more than once. That does not tell me what you should do, but it helps me understand the terrain quickly.

Practical details

Sessions take place at my practice in Sant Cugat del Vallès, Josep Puig i Cadafalch 42-44, in the Barcelona area, or online. I coach in English, French and Spanish. The rhythm, length and fee of a coaching process are agreed during the free first call, because they depend on your goal.

Frequently asked questions

Is the first call a coaching session?

No. It is a free 30-minute conversation to understand your question, check whether coaching is the right frame and agree on practical arrangements.

Coaching or therapy: how do I choose?

Coaching suits a specific professional goal: a decision, a transition, a leadership posture. Therapy works more deeply with emotional and relational experience. If you are unsure, we clarify it together in the first call.

Do you see clients from Barcelona city?

Yes. The practice is in Sant Cugat del Vallès, in the Barcelona area and reachable by public transport. Sessions can also take place online if that suits your schedule better.

In which languages do you coach?

English, French or Spanish. You can also switch language during a session, for example when work happens in one language and private life in another.

Not sure whether coaching or therapy fits?

The first free call is designed exactly for that clarification.
