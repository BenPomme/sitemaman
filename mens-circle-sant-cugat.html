The circle

A space where words can circulate

A men's circle is a regular space for meeting, speaking and exploring among men. It offers the possibility to talk about subjects that often have little room elsewhere: couple relationships, fatherhood, sexuality and intimacy, work, pressure and success, separations, loneliness, life changes, anger, fear, vulnerability, the relationship with the body, or the difficulty of knowing what one feels or wants.

There is no need to arrive with a particular problem. Some men come because they are going through a difficult period; others simply because they want to understand themselves better, step out of certain automatic patterns, or experience another way of being in relationship with other men.

The circle has no imposed theme for each session: the work is built from what the participants bring and from what emerges in the group.

How it unfolds

How does a session work?

Arriving and settling

Each meeting lasts about 2 hours. We usually begin with a moment that allows each man to settle and to become aware of what is present for him at that moment.

Letting the words circulate

Then speaking circulates from the experiences, situations or questions brought by the participants. The group can also become a space for experimentation: observing what happens in the relationship with others, putting an emotion into words, exploring a difficulty, trying another way of communicating.

Staying with what is there

Sometimes it is simply about staying with what is felt rather than immediately trying to resolve it. There is no obligation to reveal anything and no obligation to speak: each man remains responsible for his own rhythm and for what he wishes to share.

Frame and facilitation

My role in the group

I facilitate the circle as a Gestalt therapist. My role is above all to create and maintain a frame that is safe enough to allow authentic speaking: respect for each man, confidentiality, absence of judgement, and attention to what is happening in the group.

I may offer questions, draw attention to certain emotions or relational dynamics, invite slowing down or going deeper into an experience and, when it is relevant, suggest small experiments drawn in particular from Gestalt therapy.

I am not there to tell participants what they should think or do, nor to give ready-made solutions. The work consists rather in helping each man become aware of his way of being in relationship with himself and with others, and possibly to discover new possibilities.

The group over time

Why a regular group?

Much of the richness of a circle comes from the trust that builds over successive meetings. This is why participants are asked to be able to commit to a certain regularity. It is not a one-off workshop that one attends occasionally: the presence of each man progressively contributes to creating the group.

Confidentiality is essential: what is shared in the circle stays in the circle.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be going through a difficult time to join the circle?

No. Some men come because they are going through a difficult period; others simply because they want to understand themselves better, step out of certain automatic patterns or experience another way of being in relationship with other men. There is no need to arrive with a particular problem.

Where does the circle take place?

At the Esfera Gestalt centre, Av. de Rius i Taulet 29-31, 1st floor, door 4, 08172 Sant Cugat del Vallès, in the Barcelona area. Get directions on Google Maps.

Which language is used in the circle?

English or French, depending on the men interested. The exchanges take place in the language chosen by the participants of the group.

How much does a session cost?

The indicative price is 35 to 45 EUR per 2-hour session, depending on the number of participants. The final price will be set when the group is formed.

Is there a commitment for a fixed period?

No duration is fixed in advance. Participants are simply asked to be able to commit to a certain regularity, because trust builds over successive meetings. The day and the frequency will be decided with the first interested men.

I am not sure yet whether this group suits me, can we talk first?

Yes. You can contact me with no commitment: we will have a first exchange so that I can explain how the circle works, hear what you are looking for and see whether this group could suit you.
