Gestalt work with couples

What Gestalt helps you see

The work starts from an exchange that actually happens between you, not from a ready-made theory about couples.

Pause the familiar exchange

Take a recurring moment: one partner presses for a clear answer, the other goes quiet. In session, I may stop the discussion there so we can look at what is happening between you rather than replay the same debate.

Notice body, feeling and need

I ask each of you what you notice in your body, what you feel, what you need, and how you are responding to the other. Both perspectives have a place in the room.

Try one different sentence

Each partner tries one concrete change in the exchange: naming the fear beneath the pressure, saying what makes an answer difficult, or proposing a specific time to return to the subject. We then notice what changes and what does not.

Keep the focus relational

Attention to present experience, physical sensation and the relationship is central to Gestalt work. I do not apply a ready-made script; the exploration is built from what each of you brings.
