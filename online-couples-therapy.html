Couples therapy · English / French

Online couples therapy, in English

When the same argument keeps coming back, when communication wears thin or a decision weighs on you both, a space for two helps to put things back on the table. Sessions are online, in English, with both partners present at the same time.

Who is this for?

What brings a couple to therapy

You do not need to be on the brink of separation to start.

Arguments that repeat

The same reproaches, the same silences, an imbalance that has settled in and wears the relationship down.

Communication and intimacy

Difficulty saying things out loud, growing distance, sexuality, jealousy, damaged trust.

Decisions and transitions

A child, a move abroad, a return home, bereavement, or a career change that pulls you in different directions.

How sessions work

  1. Free first call. Thirty minutes to understand the request, hear both points of view and check feasibility from your country.
  2. A clear frame. We agree the rhythm, times, fee and how the online sessions will run before we start.
  3. 75 minute sessions. Each of you joins from a calm, private place with a stable connection. Both partners need to be present.
  4. Reviews. We look at how the work is going and adjust to what changes.

What this frame is not

It is not legal mediation, not an emergency service, and not therapy imposed on a reluctant partner. Sometimes only one partner wants to begin: in that case individual work is often the useful first step, and the page on online therapy describes that frame.

Therapy or coaching?

Couples therapy works on the relationship and on what keeps repeating inside it. Coaching is better suited to a specific personal or professional goal. The page Thérapie ou coaching ? sets out the difference.

Practical questions

Before you start

Should we both join the first call?

Ideally yes. If your partner is unsure, the person who gets in touch can come alone first, and we decide the next step together.

What is the fee?

It depends on your country of residence and is listed under Fees and practicalities on this page. A reduced fee is possible.

Which languages?

Sessions can be held in English or French.

How many sessions?

It depends on the situation. The first call is where we agree a realistic rhythm, often one session every two to four weeks.

Is it confidential?

Yes. What is said in session is confidential, within the limits set by professional ethics and the law.

Is this an emergency service?

No. In an emergency, or if there is violence or immediate danger, contact the emergency services in the country where you are.
