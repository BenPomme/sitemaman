Common reasons people come

Support for the emotional reality of living abroad

Living internationally can bring freedom, but also isolation, invisible stress and identity questions. Therapy can help make sense of what is happening without reducing your experience to a symptom.

Anxiety and stress

Work pressure, uncertainty, family distance, language fatigue or a nervous system that no longer feels settled.

Transitions

Moving country, changing career, becoming a parent, grieving, separating, or rebuilding a life that no longer fits.

Relationships

Patterns in couple, family or professional relationships that keep repeating and need a more spacious way of being understood.

Practical questions

Before you book

Which languages can we work in?

English, French or Spanish, depending on what feels most natural for you.

Where do sessions take place?

In person in Barcelona and Sant Cugat del Vallès (practice: Josep Puig i Cadafalch 42-44, Sant Cugat), or online depending on your situation.

How much does a session cost?

Fees are listed in the Fees and practical details section above: in-person in Barcelona / Sant Cugat and online for clients in Spain. A sliding-scale rate is possible.

Is the first call a therapy session?

No. The free 30 minutes are there to get to know each other and clarify your request and practical details; it is not a therapy session.
