For whom

A simple first step when you are not sure where to begin

People often book this first conversation when they are hesitating between therapy and coaching, living through a transition, or looking for support in a language that feels natural.

Therapy questions

Anxiety, emotional overload, relationship patterns, grief, loss of bearings or recurring difficulties.

Coaching questions

Career transition, leadership posture, decision-making, workplace stress or burnout prevention.

Expat questions

Living between languages, rebuilding support, cultural fatigue, identity shifts or family distance.

What happens during the free first call?

You briefly describe your situation and what you hope might change. Sylviane explains the possible frame, answers practical questions and helps you decide whether an individual session, coaching work or another support route would be more suitable.

Is it therapy already?

No. It is a first orientation conversation. It is confidential, but it is not a full therapeutic session. Its purpose is to help you choose the next step with more clarity.

Can sessions be online?

Yes. Sessions can take place in Sant Cugat del Vallès or online, depending on your situation and what is appropriate for the work.
